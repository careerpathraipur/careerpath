Who we are

About Career Path Job Consultant

A Raipur-based recruitment consultancy focused on connecting employers with potential workers and helping job seekers explore opportunities.

Our purpose

Career Path Job Consultant is being established to make it easier for businesses to communicate their workforce requirements and for job seekers to express interest in suitable employment opportunities.

Our focus is blue-collar recruitment across Raipur and other locations in Chhattisgarh. We aim to understand each requirement clearly and consider candidates based on the role, skills, experience and location involved.

Our mission

To make recruitment more accessible and organised by bringing employers and job seekers closer together.

Our approach

  • Listen carefully to employer requirements.
  • Understand candidate skills, experience and work preferences.
  • Communicate relevant job details as clearly as possible.
  • Treat candidates and employers respectfully and handle shared information responsibly.

Building connections, one requirement at a time

Career Path Job Consultant is a newly established consultancy. We do not claim a track record, client list or placement guarantee. Our aim is to build trust through clear communication and practical recruitment support.

Let’s discuss your requirement

We welcome enquiries from employers and job seekers across Chhattisgarh.
