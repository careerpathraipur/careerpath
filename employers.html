Looking for Workers?

Share your workforce requirements with CareerPathRaipur. We aim to identify potential candidates for roles that match your needs.

How we can help

We focus on understanding the job role, required skills, work location and expected joining timeline before considering potential candidates.

Details to include in your enquiry

  • Company or business name
  • Contact person and designation
  • Job title and number of vacancies
  • Job location
  • Required skills or experience
  • Salary or wage range
  • Working hours and employment type
  • Expected joining date
