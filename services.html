What we do

Recruitment Services

Practical recruitment support for businesses seeking blue-collar workers across Raipur and Chhattisgarh.

01

Blue-Collar Recruitment

Support for businesses looking to fill operational, field, support and other non-office roles, subject to actual hiring requirements.

02

Candidate Sourcing

We seek to identify potential candidates based on the role, required skills, experience and job location specified by the employer.

03

Hiring Requirement Coordination

We encourage employers to share clear details about vacancies, pay, work conditions and expected joining dates.

04

Candidate Matching

We aim to connect candidates with relevant openings where their profile appears to match the employer’s stated requirements.

Example job categories

These categories illustrate our intended focus; they are not current vacancies or a promise that every role is available.

Operations & Warehousing

Helpers, packers, warehouse assistants and general workers.

Logistics & Field Work

Delivery workers, drivers and other field-support roles.

Facilities & Production

Housekeeping, facility support, retail support and production workers.
